# The Silent City

No one remembers who built the towers. They rise past the clouds, past the reach of any eye, and the dust settles on their stairs as softly as snow.

For two hundred years the wind has not come here. The banners hang where they were left. The great doors stand half open, as though the people stepped out for a moment and meant to return.

If you walk the long avenue at dusk, you will hear something. Not voices, exactly. A low hum, deep in the stone, like monks chanting in a hall you cannot find.

Some say the city is waiting. Others say it is listening. I have walked those streets, and I will tell you this: it is not empty. It is only very, very patient.
